# Account Security (fictional)

If you think your account was hacked or compromised, reset your password right away at the self-service portal at https://reset.acme.example. Then contact the IT helpdesk at extension 4357 (HELP) or helpdesk@acme.example and tell them your account may be compromised.

IT staff will never ask for your password or your MFA codes. If someone asks, report it with the "Report Phish" button in Outlook.

To set up MFA (multi-factor authentication) on a new phone, install the Microsoft Authenticator app on the new phone. Then contact the IT helpdesk at extension 4357 (HELP) to move your account to it. Keep your old phone until the new one works.

If you lost the phone that has your authenticator app, contact the IT helpdesk at extension 4357 (HELP) as soon as possible.
